Support

Help with Growth Planner for iPhone and iPad.

Contact us

Email support@spirit-seven.com with any question, problem or suggestion. It helps to include your device and iOS version.

How do I get Pro on a new iPhone or iPad?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you bought Pro with. In Growth Planner, tap … then any Pro feature, such as Reach a goal, and tap Restore purchases.

Can my family use Pro?

Yes. Pro is a one-off purchase shared through Family Sharing, so people in your family group can use it on their own devices. If it doesn't appear for them, they can tap Restore purchases on the Pro screen.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. It's a single purchase, yours to keep.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my plans stored?

Only on your device. They're included in your iCloud or computer backup, like any app's data. Deleting the app deletes your plans.

How do I change the currency?

Tap … then Settings. Changing the currency changes how amounts are shown. It doesn't convert the amounts in your plans.

How are the projections worked out?

The balance grows monthly at the effective annual growth rate you enter, so 12% growth turns 1,000 into exactly 1,120 over a year. Fees are taken monthly from the balance, and today's-money figures divide by inflation to that date. Tap the ⓘ next to any input in the app for more detail.

Projections are estimates based on the assumptions you choose. They're not guaranteed and aren't financial advice.

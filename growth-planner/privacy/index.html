Growth Planner

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Growth Planner doesn't collect, store or share any personal data.

Your plans stay on your device

Everything you enter (balances, contributions, rates and goals) is stored only on your iPhone or iPad. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else. If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, your plans are included in that backup, as with any app's data.

No accounts, analytics or tracking

The app has no sign-in, contains no analytics or advertising, and doesn't track you across apps or websites.

No internet connection is needed

The app makes no network requests of its own.

Purchases

Growth Planner Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and your account details; we don't receive them. Apple's privacy policy is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Reports you share

If you share a PDF report or summary, it goes only where you choose to send it, using the share sheet.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

This website

The sections above are about the app. This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits, so we can see how people find it and improve it. It doesn't use cookies or identify you. We see totals only, such as pages visited, the site people came from, and their country, browser and device type. The calculator's figures stay in your browser and aren't sent anywhere.

You can stop your visits being counted in this browser: Don't count my visits

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security. GitHub's privacy statement is at docs.github.com.
